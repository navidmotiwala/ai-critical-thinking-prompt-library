# Advanced Review Combinations

## 1. Skeptic plus fact checker

> Review [claim] as both a skeptic and a fact checker. First identify the strongest objections, then audit supporting evidence, and finish with a calibrated conclusion.

## 2. Red team plus repair

> Stress-test [plan] for realistic failure modes. For each material weakness, propose a fix and a test proving the fix works.

## 3. Pre-mortem plus triggers

> Assume [initiative] fails. Identify likely causes, leading indicators, and exact trigger conditions for intervention.

## 4. Bias plus source audit

> Inspect [analysis] for framing, selection, incentives, and source reliability. Explain how each could change the conclusion.

## 5. Logic plus numbers

> Check the logical argument and recalculate the quantitative evidence in [report]. Flag conclusions that do not follow from the numbers.

## 6. Contradiction plus consistency

> Compare [documents] for conflicting statements, definitions, timelines, and requirements. Quote each conflict and recommend a resolution.

## 7. Decision plus uncertainty

> Recommend among [options] using evidence quality, risk, reversibility, and opportunity cost. State which new fact would change the recommendation.

## 8. Security plus usability

> Review [product design] for defensive security, privacy, accessibility, and usability tradeoffs. Prioritize fixes without providing exploitation instructions.

## 9. SEO content audit

> Review [webpage] for factual accuracy, search intent, entity consistency, source quality, misleading claims, outdated information, and AI-search readability. Separate SEO hypotheses from verified issues.

## 10. Research protocol

> Turn [research question] into hypotheses, inclusion criteria, source requirements, confounders, validation checks, and a reporting template.

## 11. Adversarial interview

> Ask ten difficult questions that would expose weaknesses in [proposal]. After my answers, score only the evidence and reasoning, not confidence or style.

## 12. Final editorial gate

> Review [output] for unsupported claims, duplicated ideas, missing caveats, vague language, excessive length, and unnatural phrasing. Return a corrected version and a short change log.
