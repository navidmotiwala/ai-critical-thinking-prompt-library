# Adversarial review prompts

### 1. Steelman the opposition

> Construct the strongest reasonable objection to [proposal]. Use accurate assumptions and realistic constraints. Then respond to that objection without dismissing it.

### 2. Decision stress test

> Evaluate [decision] under best-case, base-case, and worst-case conditions. Identify trigger points that should cause us to stop, revise, or proceed.

### 3. Red-team a product plan

> Review [product plan] from the perspectives of a user, engineer, security reviewer, support team, and finance owner. Find failure modes and conflicting incentives. Focus on safe defensive analysis rather than exploit instructions.

### 4. Opportunity cost

> Compare [option A] and [option B] against doing nothing. Include costs, reversibility, dependencies, and what must be true for each option to win.

### 5. Blind-spot audit

> Identify the stakeholders, constraints, data segments, and second-order effects missing from [analysis]. Rank omissions by their likely effect on the conclusion.
