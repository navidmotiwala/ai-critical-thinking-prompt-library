## 2. Known unknowns

## 4. Error taxonomy

> Classify mistakes in [answer] as factual, reasoning, calculation, interpretation, omission, or instruction-following errors.

## 5. Self-correction

> Re-evaluate [previous answer] using [new evidence]. State exactly which conclusions change and which remain supported.

## 6. Model limitation disclosure

> Explain which parts of [task] require external tools, real-time data, domain experts, or direct observation.

## 7. Ambiguity management

> List plausible interpretations of [question]. Answer the most likely one and flag ambiguities that would change the outcome.

## 8. Disagreement without contrarianism

> Assess [claim] independently. Challenge weak evidence, but do not oppose a well-supported conclusion just to sound critical.

## 9. Overfitting to prompt

## 10. Verification plan

> Design the fastest credible way to verify the top three uncertain claims in [answer], including expected sources or tests.
