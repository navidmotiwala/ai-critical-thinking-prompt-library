# Evidence and uncertainty prompts

### 1. Claim ledger

> Extract factual claims from [text]. Classify each as verified by provided evidence, unverified, disputed, or opinion. Identify what source or test is needed to verify each unverified claim.

### 2. Source quality

> Evaluate the sources behind [claim]. Check relevance, publication date, primary versus secondary evidence, conflicts of interest, and whether the cited material actually supports the conclusion. Do not invent citations.

### 3. Quantitative sanity check

> Recalculate all figures in [analysis]. Show formulas, denominators, date ranges, units, and rounding. Flag unsupported comparisons and misleading percentage changes.

### 4. Calibrated uncertainty

> Answer [question] using only the available evidence. State what is known, what is inferred, and what remains unknown. If the evidence is insufficient, say so and specify the next best verification step.

### 5. Falsification test

> What observation, experiment, or dataset would most strongly disprove [hypothesis]? Design a practical test and explain possible confounding factors.
