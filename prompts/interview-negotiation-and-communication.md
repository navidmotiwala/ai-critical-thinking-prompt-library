# Interview Negotiation And Communication

## 1. Interview answer challenge

> Review [interview answer] for vague claims, unsupported achievements, missing examples, and questions a skeptical interviewer would ask.

## 2. Resume evidence

> Audit [resume bullets] for inflated impact, missing baselines, unverifiable numbers, and ambiguous ownership.

## 3. Negotiation assumptions

> Evaluate [negotiation plan] for alternatives, reservation points, leverage, relationship costs, and untested assumptions.

## 4. Stakeholder disagreement

> Draft a respectful challenge to [proposal] that names evidence gaps, business impact, and a constructive next step.

## 5. Meeting decision quality

> Review [meeting notes] for unresolved decisions, conflicting owners, missing deadlines, and assumptions presented as agreement.

## 6. Client report scrutiny

> Act as a skeptical client reading [report]. Identify what results mean, what is unproven, and which questions remain unanswered.

## 7. Difficult feedback

> Rewrite [feedback] to be specific, fair, evidence-based, and actionable without flattery or hostility.

## 8. Executive summary test

> Condense [analysis] to decision, evidence, risks, and requested action. Remove background that does not change the decision.

## 9. Cross-examination

> Ask one difficult question at a time about [proposal]. After each answer, identify unsupported assertions and request concrete evidence.

## 10. Disagreement calibration

> Write a disagreement with [claim] that accurately states the other person's position and avoids straw-man arguments.
