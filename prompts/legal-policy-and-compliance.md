# Legal Policy And Compliance

## 1. Policy interpretation

> Analyze [policy] by separating explicit requirements, reasonable interpretations, and unanswered questions. Cite the relevant clauses.

## 2. Contract ambiguity

> Review [contract clause] for vague terms, conflicting obligations, missing remedies, and operational consequences. Do not present legal conclusions as certainty.

## 3. Regulatory freshness

> Identify which parts of [compliance advice] require current jurisdiction-specific verification and primary regulatory sources.

## 4. Audit trail

> Evaluate [decision process] for approvals, version history, evidence retention, access logs, and accountability gaps.

## 5. Consent design

> Review [consent flow] for clear choices, revocation, purpose specificity, and potentially coercive interface design.

## 6. Claims substantiation

> Check [marketing claims] for measurable definitions, substantiating evidence, qualifiers, and misleading comparisons.

## 7. Cross-border assumptions

> Identify legal, data-transfer, tax, and operational questions in [international plan] that require local expert review.

## 8. Accessibility requirements

> Review [digital experience] against relevant accessibility criteria and identify testable issues, not unsupported compliance labels.

## 9. Policy conflict

> Compare [internal policies] for contradictory instructions, unclear precedence, and missing exception handling.

## 10. Compliance handoff

> Create a checklist of facts, documents, and questions a qualified adviser needs to assess [issue].
