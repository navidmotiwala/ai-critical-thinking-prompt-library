# Security Privacy And Safety

## 1. Threat model

> Create a defensive threat model for [system] listing assets, trust boundaries, likely attacker goals, abuse scenarios, mitigations, and verification tests. Avoid exploit steps.

## 2. Prompt injection defense

> Review [AI workflow] for untrusted retrieved content, instruction hierarchy confusion, tool permission misuse, and unsafe data exfiltration paths. Recommend defensive controls.

## 3. Sensitive data exposure

> Identify where [workflow] may collect, store, log, display, or transmit sensitive information unnecessarily. Recommend minimization and retention controls.

## 4. Access control review

> Check [application specification] for missing authorization checks, privilege escalation risks, tenant isolation, and audit requirements.

## 5. Supply chain review

> Audit [dependency plan] for provenance, pinning, vulnerability response, license compatibility, and update cadence.

## 6. Secrets hygiene

> Review [deployment process] for embedded credentials, exposed environment variables, excessive permissions, and unsafe secret rotation practices.

## 7. Abuse cases

> Generate realistic misuse scenarios for [feature], rate their impact, and propose safeguards that preserve legitimate use.

## 8. Incident readiness

> Review [incident response plan] for detection, ownership, escalation, containment, recovery, communication, and lessons learned.

## 9. Privacy by design

> Evaluate [product] against data minimization, consent clarity, purpose limitation, retention, deletion, and user controls.

## 10. Safety boundary audit

> Assess [AI feature] for harmful failure modes, overconfidence, human oversight, and fallback behavior when evidence is insufficient.
