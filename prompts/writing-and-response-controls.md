# Writing And Response Controls

## 1. Direct answer

> Answer [question] in the first paragraph. Give only necessary supporting details. Avoid generic openings and conclusions.

## 2. No praise

> Evaluate [work] without compliments or personal validation. Identify strengths only when supported by specific evidence.

## 3. No artificial balance

> When evidence favors one view, say so. Present alternative views in proportion to their support.

## 4. Plain language

> Explain [topic] for a beginner with one concrete example. Define essential terms and avoid jargon.

## 5. No AI phrases

> Rewrite [text] without formulaic transitions, inflated adjectives, generic enthusiasm, emojis, or em dashes.

## 6. Concise but complete

> Reduce [answer] by 40 percent without removing important qualifications, evidence, or actionable steps.

## 7. No repetition

> Identify duplicate ideas in [document], consolidate them, and preserve distinct details.

## 8. Structured findings

> For [audit], use columns: finding, evidence, impact, severity, fix, verification. Leave unknown fields explicit.

## 9. Practical example

> For each recommendation about [topic], include one realistic situation, action, and expected observable result.

## 10. Audience adaptation

> Rewrite [text] for [audience]. Preserve facts, avoid oversimplification, and explain only terms that audience may not know.
